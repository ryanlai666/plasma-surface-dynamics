# SiO2 / HF_HFv1

Source: [Jung et al. (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1116/1.5125569), Table I and Section II.

- Cluster: Si8O12H3F11, strongly fluorinated surface-site model.
- Site equation: `theta3 -> EP + theta1a`; EP denotes lumped etch products, not a balanced molecular equation.
- Activation parameter: 8061 K = 0.694643 eV above the model reactant reference.
- Prefactor: 6.55e+08 s^-1.
- R7 nitride uses a 5800 K vibrational-energy subtraction from ground-state 7556 K; not a separate excited-state saddle search.
- FS energy and matched Cartesian paths were not recovered; no continuous path or GIF is claimed.

The source reports IRC connectivity and one imaginary frequency for its computed TSs. The vibrationally assisted nitride value also assumes excitation survives long enough to promote reaction. These conditions are not transferable to arbitrary nitride composition.
